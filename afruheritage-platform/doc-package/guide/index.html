Start here

What this is. Afruheritage is a freight-forwarding platform. During the pilot it is invitation-only: you can only create an account with an email address the Afruheritage team has invited.

Where things live.

What you wantWhere
Customer website (register, sign in, ship, track, calculate duties)https://afruheritage.com
Your company portal (after registering a business)Shown in your welcome email and on your dashboard
Internal admin console (Afruheritage staff only)https://admin.afruheritage.com/sentinel
Pilot note: account emails currently arrive from advisory@emidentity.com while the Afruheritage sender address is being finalized. Those emails are genuine.
No codes to type. Afruheritage does not send one-time codes (OTP). You either sign in with your password, or you click a link in an email (invitations and password resets).

Sentinel Administrator (internal staff only)

Sign in

  1. Open https://admin.afruheritage.com/sentinel/login.
  2. Enter your Email and Password, then click Sign in.
  3. You land on the Dashboard — a summary of tenants, vendors, and shipments.
It worked when: you see the dashboard cards and the left-hand menu (Dashboard, Tenants, Shipments, Tickets, Tracking, Vendors, Users, Billing, Payments, Control Center, Feature Flags, Domains, Diagnostics, Analytics).

Admit and invite a pilot customer

Important: adding an email to the invitation-only list is currently a developer-run command, not a Sentinel button. Ask the platform team to admit the customer's email; they record it in the admissions log. There is no screen in Sentinel today for this step — do not look for one.

Once the email is admitted, the customer registers themselves on afruheritage.com (see "Individual Customer" or "Business Owner" below). Sentinel does not create the account for them.

Check whether a customer registered

  1. Click Tenants in the left menu.
  2. Use the search box to type the customer's company name or email.
  3. The row shows their status (for example pending approval, active) and their portal address.
It worked when: the tenant appears in the list with the expected company name and portal URL.
If the tenant is missing: the customer has not finished registering, or they used a different email. Check with them first; check the admissions log if you suspect the email was never admitted.

Approve an account

  1. In Tenants, find the tenant and open their row.
  2. Use the Approve action where the tenant is waiting for review. The tenant's status changes and their portal is prepared.

Manage staff (admin users)

  1. Click Users in the left menu (super administrators only).
  2. Click Add user (or the create control shown) and enter the staff member's name, email, password, and role (admin or super_admin).
  3. To disable someone, open their row and deactivate them.
It worked when: the new user appears in the list. Viewer-level admins can see this page but get "Forbidden" if they try to create users — that is expected.

Review shipments

  1. Click Shipments in the left menu to see shipments across the platform.
  2. Open a shipment to see its tracking history and notes.

Handle support tickets

  1. Click Tickets in the left menu.
  2. Open a ticket to read the conversation, then write a reply and send it.
  3. Change the ticket status (for example to resolved) with the status control.

Review vendors

  1. Click Vendors in the left menu to see delivery vendors who registered.
  2. Open a vendor to review their details, then approve or reject their application.

Check problems (diagnostics and audit)

  1. Click Diagnostics to see system health and the audit log — who did what and when.
  2. Click Analytics for platform usage summaries.

Individual Customer — full journey, verified live

Every screen below was captured during a real registration and sign-in on the pilot system.

Step A — Open the registration page

  1. Open https://afruheritage.com and click Get Started, or go straight to https://afruheritage.com/register.
  2. You see the page "Start Your Freight Forwarding Journey" with social buttons on top ("Continue with Google / Instagram / TikTok") and, below the divider "OR REGISTER WITH EMAIL", the email form.

Step B — Fill the form

  1. Under "I am signing up as" click Individual (it stays highlighted). Choosing Business adds a required Company Name field.
  2. Type your Full Name.
  3. Type the Email that was admitted to the pilot — it must be the same address.
  4. Type a Password (at least 8 characters) and repeat it in Confirm Password. The eye icon toggles visibility.
  5. Click Create Account — the button shows "Creating account…" while it works.
"Passwords do not match" — retype both password fields identically. "at least 8 characters" — use a longer password. "registration is limited to invited participants" — your email is not admitted; ask your Afruheritage contact. "account already exists" — sign in instead, or use Forgot password.

Step C — Choose your plan

  1. A dialog titled "Choose Your Plan" appears — your account is already created at this point. Four cards are offered: Free Trial (14 days) — 0 GHS, Starter — 1,000 GHS/month, Growth — 2,500 GHS/month, Enterprise — 6,000 GHS/month.
  2. Click the Free Trial card (a filled radio dot marks it), then click Continue at the bottom right.
  3. For the free trial, a second dialog offers a storefront template — pick one and click Launch Storefront, or click Skip.
Known pilot defect (U-004): during the verified run, clicking Continue on the plan step briefly showed an error banner ("Failed to initialize payment: Unexpected token…") instead of the template step. Your account is still created. If this happens, close the dialog (Cancel), sign in normally, and continue — the free trial can be activated by support. Paid plans are excluded from the free pilot.

Step D — Check your welcome email

  1. An email arrives from advisory@emidentity.com with the subject "Welcome to Afruheritage — Your Account is Ready!". It confirms your sign-in email and portal address. No code is needed — Afruheritage does not use one-time passcodes.
No email after a few minutes: check spam. Still nothing? Your account still works — go sign in.

Step E — Sign in

  1. Open https://afruheritage.com/login. You see "Welcome Back".
  2. Enter your Email and Password, then click Sign In. ("Remember me" is optional.)
  3. The button shows "Signing in…" for a few seconds, then the page changes.
"Incorrect email or password" — retype carefully or use Forgot password? (top right of the password field). "Too many login attempts" — wait about a minute and try again.

Step F — First-time setup wizard

  1. New accounts land on "Welcome to Afruheritage" with a progress bar: 1 Welcome → 2 Your Role → 3 Profile → 4 Done. Click Get Started.
  2. On "What brings you to Afruheritage?", click the card that fits you — Personal Shipper (Free), Delivery Driver (KYC Required), or Freight Company (Subscription) — then click Continue.
  3. On "Complete your profile", confirm your Full Name (your sign-in email is shown above it) and click Finish Setup.

Step G — Your dashboard

  1. The dashboard greets you: "Welcome back, <your name>".
It worked when: you see quick-action cards (My Shipments, Create Shipment, Track Package, Support, Settings, Storefront Templates) and the left sidebar menu. You are fully registered and signed in.

Business Owner

Register your company

  1. Open https://afruheritage.com/register.
  2. Choose Business under "I am signing up as".
  3. Fill in your name, email, and password, plus the company details the form asks for (company name and related fields).
  4. Submit the form. The "Choose Your Plan" dialog appears — pick Free Trial and click Continue (see the U-004 note in Individual Customer if it errors; your account is still created).
  5. Your company workspace is created, and you get a company portal address (for example your-company.afruheritage.com) in your welcome email and on your dashboard.
  6. Sign in — the first sign-in runs the same Welcome → Your Role → Profile → Done wizard as Individual, then your sidebar shows: Dashboard, Products, Shipments, Fleet, CRM, Customs Duty, Team Members, KYC, Support, Billing, Settings, Templates, Plugins, Gallery, Storefront, Profile.
It worked when: the dashboard greets you by name and the sidebar shows the company menu.

Invite team members

  1. Click Team Members in the sidebar.
  2. Click Invite Member (top right).
  3. Enter the person's email, name, and choose their role (for example Customer — read-only, or Driver).
  4. Send the invitation. They receive an email titled "You've been invited to join [your company] on Afruheritage".
It worked when: the invitation shows as pending in your team list, and the person appears as a member after they accept.
There is also a Bulk Import CSV button for inviting many people at once.

Company Staff (accepting an invitation)

  1. Open the invitation email (from advisory@emidentity.com during the pilot) and click the accept link.
  2. The accept page opens. Enter your full name and choose a password (at least 8 characters).
  3. Submit — your account is activated and you are signed in to your company's workspace.
It worked when: you see the company dashboard. Staff see the workspace but cannot invite other people.
If the link says the invitation is invalid or expired: ask the person who invited you to send a new one — invitation links expire and can only be used once.

Vendor (delivery provider)

Apply to become a vendor

  1. Open https://afruheritage.com/vendor/register.
  2. Complete the four sections:
    • Personal details — full name, email, phone, ID type (for example Ghana Card), ID number.
    • Vehicle — tick your vehicle types (Truck, Car, Motorbike, Bicycle), registration number, make/model, year.
    • Business — business name, type, regions you operate in, experience.
    • Agreement — tick the terms, insurance, and background-check boxes.
  3. Submit. You see a confirmation — applications are reviewed by the Afruheritage team (they aim for 2–3 business days).
  4. When approved, you get an email with a vendor-accept link. Click it, set your password, and your vendor account activates.
  5. Sign in — your vendor area shows your profile and bookings.
It worked when: signing in opens your vendor profile showing "approved" status and an empty bookings list to start.
If your registration is rejected: the review email explains why. Fix the issue and register again.

Driver

Drivers join a company through a team invitation (the owner invites you with the Driver role) — same steps as Company Staff above.

Once in, drivers can see open marketplace jobs near them, accept a job, update its tracking status, and share GPS position from the field. During the pilot the driver experience is exercised through the API and the marketplace flow; a dedicated driver app is on the roadmap (see Pilot Limits).

Shipments & Tracking

Create a shipment (signed-in business or individual)

  1. Sign in and click Shipments in the sidebar, then New Shipment.
  2. Fill the form — Tracking Number can stay blank ("Leave blank to auto-generate"), plus cargo type, package count, weight, and addresses.
  3. Save. The shipment appears in your list with an auto-generated tracking number like AFR-2026-000006.
It worked when: the shipment is listed and shows its tracking number and current status.

Track a shipment (anyone — no account needed)

  1. Open the tracking page (linked from the home page or /track).
  2. Type the tracking number into "Enter tracking number" and click Track.
  3. The page shows the shipment's current status, location updates, and estimated delivery.
It worked when: you see the status timeline. If the number is wrong you get a "not found" message — check the number with the sender.

Customs Duty Calculators (Ghana & Kenya)

  1. Open Customs Duty in the sidebar (signed in), or the public duty-calculator page.
  2. Pick the country — Ghana or Kenya — and what you're importing (vehicle or goods).
  3. Fill in the details (for a vehicle: make, model, year, engine size, fuel type, value). Click the calculate button.
  4. The estimate shows the duty breakdown in local currency (GHS or KES).
Pilot rules: guests (not signed in) get 2 free estimates per browser — after that you'll be asked to sign in. These are estimates only; they are not certified for paid reliance during the pilot. Ghana repricing data and the Kenya CRSP vehicle values are live but exchange rates are set manually.

Support & Account Recovery

Open a support ticket

  1. Open the Support page (from the site menu, or Support in the sidebar when signed in).
  2. Under "Create a Support Ticket" enter your name, email, subject, and what's wrong, then submit.
  3. You get a tracking token — keep it. Under "Track Your Ticket" paste the token any time to see replies and the status.

Reset a forgotten password

  1. On the sign-in page click Forgot password (or open /forgot-password).
  2. Enter your email and submit. If the account exists, a Password Reset email arrives.
  3. Click the link in the email, choose a new password, and sign in.
It worked when: after setting the new password you can sign in normally.

Postman API Testing

For testers. The UAT pack contains three files — import all of them:

FileWhat it is
AFRUHERITAGE_UAT.postman_collection.jsonThe test suite (79 requests in dependency order)
AFRUHERITAGE_UAT.postman_environment.jsonEnvironment with account emails — passwords are blank
POSTMAN_SIMPLE_START_GUIDE.mdStep-by-step instructions
  1. Get the UAT passwords from the secure credential handover (delivered separately — never in this package).
  2. In Postman, import the collection and the environment.
  3. Select the Afruheritage UAT Environment, open it, and paste each password into the matching field.
  4. Click Run collection → Run Afruheritage UAT. Requests run in order; sign-ins and IDs are handled automatically.
  5. Check the results — every request should show PASS. Anything else is a defect to report.
