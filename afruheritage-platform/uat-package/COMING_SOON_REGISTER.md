# Afruheritage — Launch Exclusions & Under Implementation

**PO rulings D-01..D-12 applied.** The capabilities below are either excluded from the initial launch or approved for build but not yet deployed. Do not test them. Known defects you SHOULD report are in DELIVERY_GAPS_AND_DEFECTS.md. Full scope: LAUNCH_SCOPE_REGISTER.md.

## Vendor marketplace & vendor registration
- **Where it appears:** Public site, help assistant, storefront marketplace section
- **Status:** Under implementation (D-01) — not yet deployed
- **Note:** Approved for build: vendor registration, approval, marketplace bookings. Not yet usable — do not test.

## Central Afruheritage platform support intake
- **Where it appears:** /support on the main site
- **Status:** Under implementation (D-04)
- **Note:** A platform-level support channel is being built. Today, tickets are submitted on each company's portal page.

## CRM activity history
- **Where it appears:** CRM section — contacts and quotes available
- **Status:** Under implementation (D-08)
- **Note:** The activity interface is being added. Contacts and quotes already work.

## Live payment collection
- **Where it appears:** Billing
- **Status:** Pending sandbox certification (D-05)
- **Note:** Manual-renewal billing model — there are no automatic recurring charges. Plan/trial selection works; do not attempt real-money payments during UAT.

## Driver portal (driver sign-in)
- **Where it appears:** Fleet management
- **Status:** Excluded from initial launch (D-02)
- **Note:** Drivers are managed records — creation and assignment work; drivers cannot sign in.

## Warehouse operations
- **Where it appears:** Not in customer navigation
- **Status:** Excluded from offering (D-06)
- **Note:** Not part of the Afruheritage launch product.

## Dedicated analytics dashboards
- **Where it appears:** Not in customer navigation
- **Status:** Excluded from initial launch (D-07)
- **Note:** Basic operational counts already work; advanced dashboards follow later.

## Additional storefront templates (mall, ecommerce, restaurant, bookings, real estate)
- **Where it appears:** Templates page
- **Status:** Excluded — publish-blocked (D-09)
- **Note:** Preview only; cannot be published until tenant-configurable content is complete.

## Custom domains

- **Note:** Company portals run on yourname.afruheritage.com. Custom domains need separate DNS/certificate work.

## Mobile app (iOS/Android)
