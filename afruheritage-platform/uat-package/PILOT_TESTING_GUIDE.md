# Afruheritage Pilot — Participant Testing Guide

Welcome to the Afruheritage invitation-only pilot. This guide explains
what to try, what should happen, and how to report problems.

## Before you start

- **Register with the exact email your invitation was sent to** —
  only invited emails can create accounts.
- Register at: https://afruheritage.com/register
- Choose **Individual** if you are testing personally, or **Business**
  if you are testing as a company.
- Emails from Afruheritage arrive from **advisory@emidentity.com**
  during the pilot — that is expected and safe.

## What to try

1. **Register and log in** — create your account, log out, log back in.
2. **Explore your dashboard** — check your workspace, wallet, and plan.
3. **Create a shipment** — add a real shipment and note the tracking
   number (format `AFR-XXXXXX-…`).
4. **Track it publicly** — use the tracking number at
   https://afruheritage.com/track without logging in.
5. **Update the shipment** — add a status update; you should receive an
   email notification.
6. **Invite a teammate** (business accounts with a plan) — send a team
   invitation, check the email arrives, accept it.
7. **Ask for help** — use the in-app support or the public ticket link
   in your notification emails; a reply should reach your mailbox.
8. **Try account recovery** — log out and use "Forgot password"; a
   reset email should arrive within a minute.

## What is NOT in the pilot

- **Payments are off** — no real charges will occur. Subscription plans
  during the pilot are free/trial-based.
- Flutterwave is not available; Paystack checkout is not enabled for
  real charges.

## Reporting a problem

Use the in-app **Support** section, or reply to any notification email.
Please include: what you tried, what you expected, what happened, and
the approximate time. Screenshots help.

## What we are measuring

Whether you can register, understand the product, create and track
shipments, receive notifications, and get support — **without help
from our team**. Confusion is data: if something was unclear, tell us.
