REG-01 Registration

Create a new business account

Who: Public Visitor → New Business Owner   Start: https://afruheritage.com

Steps & expected result
  1. Open https://afruheritage.com in your browser.
  2. Click 'Get Started' in the top-right corner.
  3. Fill in the form: your company name, your full name, your email address, and a password (at least 8 characters, entered twice).
  4. Click 'Create Account'.
  5. A 'Choose Your Plan' screen appears — select 'Free Trial' and click 'Continue'. (Activating the trial can take up to a minute; wait for the next screen.)
  6. A 'Choose Your Storefront Template' screen appears — pick a template and click 'Launch Storefront' (or 'Skip' to decide later).
  7. You are taken to the Sign In page — that means registration worked.

Expected: Your company account is created, you picked a plan and a storefront template, and you land on the Sign In page.

REG-02 Registration

Receive the welcome email

Who: New Business Owner   Start: Your email inbox

Steps & expected result
  1. Open the inbox of the email address you registered with.
  2. Look for an email titled 'Welcome to Afruheritage — <your company> is Ready!'.
  3. Open the email and check that it contains your company portal link (https://<your-company>.afruheritage.com).
  4. Note: during this pilot, platform emails are sent from 'advisory@emidentity.com' on behalf of Afruheritage — that is expected, not a fake email.

Expected: A welcome email arrives within a few minutes, addressed to your company, with a working portal link. (On the free trial you should NOT receive a payment email — no payment was made.)

REG-03 Registration

First sign-in and onboarding

Who: New Business Owner   Start: https://afruheritage.com/login

Steps & expected result
  1. Go to https://afruheritage.com and click 'Sign In' (or continue from REG-01).
  2. Enter the email and password you registered with and click 'Sign In'.
  3. On the welcome screen, click 'Get Started'.
  4. Choose 'Freight Company' and click 'Continue'.
  5. Confirm your full name and click 'Finish Setup'.

Expected: You land on your company dashboard showing your business name in the top bar. (Choosing 'Freight Company' takes you straight to the workspace — you do NOT need to fill in a second company form.)

SHIP-01 Shipments

Create a shipment

Who: Business Owner   Start: Company dashboard

Steps & expected result
  1. Click 'Shipments' in the left menu, then 'New Shipment' (or 'Create Shipment').
  2. Fill in the sender name, phone, city and country.
  3. Fill in the receiver name, phone, city and country.
  4. Describe the cargo, enter weight and number of packages, and the cost.
  5. Click 'Create Shipment'.

Expected: The shipment is created and a tracking number starting with 'AFR-' is shown.

  2. Click each status button as it appears: 'Mark as Booked', 'Mark as Picked Up', 'Mark as In Transit', 'Mark as At Customs', 'Mark as Customs Cleared', 'Mark as Out for Delivery', 'Mark as Delivered'.

Expected: The shipment moves through every stage and finishes as 'Delivered'.

FLT-01 Fleet

Register a driver

Who: Business Owner   Start: Company dashboard

Steps & expected result
  1. Click 'Fleet' in the left menu, then open 'Drivers'.
  2. Click '+ Add Driver'.
  3. Enter the driver's full name, phone number and email; optionally a city and 2-letter country code.
  4. Click 'Create Driver'.
  5. Note: use a phone number that belongs to this driver — if the phone number is already known to the platform, the driver may be linked to that existing record instead of creating a new one.

Expected: The driver appears in the drivers list with the name you entered.

ACCT-01 Account

Reset a forgotten password

Who: Any signed-in user   Start: https://afruheritage.com/login

Steps & expected result
  1. On the Sign In page, click 'Forgot Password'.
  2. Enter your account email and submit.
  3. Open the reset email (subject: 'Afruheritage — Password Reset Request').

  5. Sign in with the new password; confirm the old password no longer works.

Expected: The reset email arrives, the link works, and only the new password signs you in.
