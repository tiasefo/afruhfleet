Global acceptance criteria:
- A freight-forwarding business can sign up, choose plan/add-ons, complete payment or free-tier activation, and automatically receive an isolated runtime.
- A vendor/driver/rider can subscribe to control-plane mode without website creation.
- Admin is spectator-first and intervenes only on failure or policy events.
- Ollama is a connected dependency, not a later placeholder.
